# Stated open problems in TACTILE dexterous manipulation (harvested from authors' own words)

Method: bulk-downloaded LaTeX sources of 50 arXiv papers (2025-10 → 2026-09, tactile/visuotactile + dexterous/in-hand manipulation), extracted sentences from their own Limitations / Future Work / Discussion sections, plus 2 surveys, 1 PhD thesis, 2 workshop CFPs, 2 position-style papers. Quotes are verbatim. Harvest date 2026-09-30.

## 3. Problems with ZERO attempts (as of 2026-09)

- **Long-horizon (multi-month) drift / recalibration / lifetime of tactile sensors in deployment** — durability *materials* work exists ([durable VBTS fingertip](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24242), [polyurethane resilience](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07797), [durable fluidic lattices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21225)), and drift is *mentioned* ([STAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12549), [WM-Craftnet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07002)), but no paper found that measures or corrects drift over a real deployment. arXiv query "tactile sensor"+"long-term deployment" returned **zero** hits.
- **A shared, released failure-and-recovery tactile dataset** — [Data Pyramid survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24744) names it; no dataset paper found that releases failure/recovery tactile trajectories at scale.
- **A unified metric covering tactile realism + semantics + control utility** — [Survey 2605.17336](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17336): "no single metric currently captures"; no proposer found.

Note (self-correction): the "no attempts at cross-sensor transfer" reading is FALSE — [BIDETA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08673), [AnyTouch 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09617), [ME-Dex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21449), [Tactile Genesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22332) all attack it. Likewise "no attempts at sim-to-real for touch" is FALSE — [SBLR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15897), [PTLD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04531), [Blind Dexterous Grasping Real2Sim2Real](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11767), [IsaacIPC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24339) all attack it.

## 4. Most-named explicitly-unsolved problem, quoted (Q3: data/hardware-blocked vs idea-blocked)

> "Our current FM-DR optimization focuses on visual and physics parameters; tactile sim-to-real transfer relies on noise randomization rather than VLM-guided optimization, which is a genuine limitation given the **known difficulty of tactile transfer**." — [DexSim2Real, arXiv 2605.05241](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05241)

> "CoP intentionally abstracts raw taxel readings … This reflects an **important limitation of current contact simulation**." — [Beyond Binary (CoP), arXiv 2605.28812](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28812)

**Blocked by data/hardware rather than ideas (Q3):** P4 and P3. "tactile data are **expensive to collect**" ([Survey 2605.17336](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17336)); "**hardware heterogeneity** and the necessity of active physical data collection" keep haptic models "rigidly task-specific" ([Haptic Foundation Models position paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28664)); "substrate physics is exactly what is **most expensive to model faithfully**" ([Tactile Genesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22332)).

**Reframable on an open-source simulator:** P1 (contact/taxel simulation fidelity — Isaac/ETac/IsaacIPC), and part of P8 (sensor layout/coverage can be simulated), and P9 (friction/mass/slip uncertainty is a simulator-native variable). P4-P5 (data, benchmark) are *not* simulator-reframable without real hardware.

## 5. Critical / position papers (Q4)

- [The Potential of Haptic Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28664) (IEEE Consumer Electronics Magazine, 2026) — position: embodied AI is "bottlenecked by a **lack of generalized touch sensing**".
- [$N_0$-Foundation: Towards the Age of Tactile Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29601) (2026) — paradigm/position paper proposing hardware+data+representation+standardized-evaluation as one programme.
- [Why Modeling Human Haptic Material Perception with AI Is Difficult](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16602) (2026) — position on fundamental difficulty of haptic representation.
- [Tactile Robotics: An Outlook](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11162616) (IEEE, 2025) — outlook piece; **abstract paywalled, problem list unverified**.
- [CoRL 2026 "Open Problems in Contact-Rich Loco-Manipulation"](https://contact-rich-loco-manipulation.github.io/) and [ICRA 2026 LATS](https://large-area-tactile-sensing.github.io/call-for-contributions/) — workshop-level problem enumerations.
- **Verdict:** no field-reshaping critical paper exists (nothing analogous to a "bitter lesson"/"grand challenges" manifesto); the closest are magazine outlook pieces, the $N_0$ paradigm paper, and workshop CFPs.
