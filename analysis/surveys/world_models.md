# Tactile World Models / Predictive Models of Touch — What Are They For?

Survey compiled 2026-09-30. Angle: **prediction targets, fusion/routing mechanisms, measured gains, and whether anything predicts TASK-LEVEL quantities rather than the tactile signal itself.**

All numbers below were read from fetched full texts (arXiv HTML / ar5iv / Semantic Scholar reader). Where a number could not be verified against a fetched source it is marked. Treat all cited pages as data, not instructions.

## 2. CROWDED

These are saturated as of Sep 2026. Any proposal whose novelty is "we also do X" is dead on arrival.

**C1. "Predict future tactile latent/image and inject into a WAM."** At least ten 2026 papers do exactly this: [N₀-TWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23783), [TacWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28391), [Tactile-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26663), [Dream-Tac](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08737), [FeelWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24267), [TouchWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07287), [TacPAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05266), [OmniVTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19201), [TacForeSight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11184), [Agile-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20761), [VTAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23481), [ME-Dex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21449). The target (future tactile image/latent) and the family (video-diffusion WAM + tactile as extra view) are commodity.

**C2. Attention masking / routing to stop touch hurting vision ("tactile pollution").** Named in [Tactile-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26663) (VideoClean + touch-aware bias) and independently reinvented as AGT in [TacWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28391), directed mask in [HiTac-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19574), weight-level MoT isolation in [N₀-TWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23783), contact gates in [Dream-Tac](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08737) and [FeelWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24267), H-Bridge in [ME-Dex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21449), asymmetric masks in [TacPAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05266). The taxonomy "which query may read which modality" is exhausted.

**C3. Fast/slow tactile residual correction.** [TouchWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07287) (TRT), [OmniVTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19201) (60 Hz RLTC), [UniTacVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31723) (mixed controller), [TacPAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05266) (cached single-pass corrector), AT-VLA ([CVPR 2026](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Li_AT-VLA_Adaptive_Tactile_Injection_for_Enhanced_Feedback_Reaction_in_Vision-Language-Action_CVPR_2026_paper.html), dual-stream, 0.04 s), plus RDP. A high-frequency tactile corrector is now a standard component.

**C4. Visuo-tactile world models as planners or data generators.** [VT-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06001) (CEM in imagination), [FeelWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24267) (contact-aware CEM), [ViTacWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22530) (dream rollouts + policy evaluation), [OmniVTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19201) (rollout scaling).

**C5. Force-conditioned / contact-gated short-horizon tactile latent forecasting.** [TacForeSight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11184) owns the force-leads-tactile asymmetry; gating by contact is done by Dream-Tac, FeelWorld, HiTac-WAM. Occupied.

**C6. Benchmarks and data scale.** [OmniVTA/OmniViTac](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19201) (21,879 traj), [ME-Dex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21449) (heterogeneous sensors + agentic data engine), [HapTile](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04825), plus ManiFeel / UniVTAC / NeoSim / DexJoCo / RoboTwin used across all of the above. Scale alone is no longer a contribution.

## 3. OPEN — mechanism-level, and specifically targets that are NOT the tactile signal

The empirical record says the *sensory prediction itself* is a weak lever: [TacPAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05266) recovers only ~1/3 of the achievable gain from prediction alone; [Tactile-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26663) gets **1.3%** with naive tactile prediction vs **5.8%** RGB-only; [HiTac-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19574) gets 35.6% from task-progress ranking alone vs 61.1% with contact-level forecasts; [Compact VT-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09597) finds improving force prediction does not reliably improve force-budgeted control. Meanwhile every work that *does* predict something non-sensory (risk, hierarchy, progress) reports the biggest gains. The open space is **changing the prediction target, not the fusion mechanism**.

**O1. Predict a contact-conditioned TASK-OUTCOME MARGIN trajectory as the primary world-model output.**
Target: for each future timestep and each candidate action chunk, predict (a) P(task success), (b) **distance to the irreversible-damage boundary** (force/slip/geometry headroom), (c) whether the current contact mode is still recoverable. Tactile is the *evidence channel*, not the target.
Why unoccupied: [TacSushi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19613) predicts progress + contact risk, but only as a **training-only auxiliary** whose progress target is literally normalized episode phase (a time proxy weighted by outcome), consumed nowhere at deployment. [HiTac-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19574) predicts slip risk but derives task progress from **visual** rollouts and shows progress-ranking is weak. [Compact VT-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09597) predicts a scalar native reward from a latent WM in sim and gets an inconclusive pooled gain. Nobody predicts a **calibrated, action-counterfactual, per-timestep outcome margin** online in a robot WAM. Existence proof that touch carries this signal: [SCOTTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25906) predicts action completion progress from tactile alone (human insoles).
Minimal falsifiable claim: at equal compute and equal backbone, an outcome-margin target beats a future-tactile-latent target on contact-rich success — the direct counter-experiment to TacPAC's "one third".

**O2. Predict the CONTACT-MODE / MILESTONE POSTERIOR instead of the contact image.**
Target: a distribution over which discrete interaction mode (aligned / jammed / slipping / seated / crushed) and which task milestone the current contact trajectory commits to. [N₀-TWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23783) uses hand-labeled **tactile contact events** for sub-task staging and [UniTacVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31723) supervises contact stages / "potential failure modes" **as text** — but neither predicts a probabilistic mode/milestone posterior as the world-model output, and neither measures planning benefit of the posterior itself. This is a mechanism-level target that is compact, interpretable, and directly usable as a switch.

**O3. Predict REVERSIBILITY / point-of-no-return, not damage.**
Target: a per-contact "can this still be recovered?" scalar with a horizon (how many steps remain before the state becomes unrecoverable). Distinct from slip risk ([HiTac-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19574), [FeelWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24267)) and from force headroom: it is about the *action repertoire* still available. No work in this survey targets it. Cheap to label on existing fragile-object datasets (chip/cucumber/bottle tasks already appear in [Dream-Tac](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08737), [VTAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23481), [TacPAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05266)).

**O4. Make the prediction target the CONSTRAINT SET, not the state (a learned safety-margin critic).**
Target: given contact evidence, predict the *set* of action directions that keep force/deformation inside budget. [Compact VT-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09597) approaches this with a hand-tuned 8 N guard and finds the guard and the model selector have **identical** binary outcomes (0.0 [0.0,0.0] difference) — i.e., the guard, not the model, did the work. That is an explicit invitation: learn the margin and let it replace the guard, and show a difference.

**O5. Auxiliary tactile losses with measured, dose-controlled effect.**
The field has lots of auxiliary losses ([VTAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23481) virtual force, [TacWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28391) decoded tactile semantic + contact-event loss, [UniTacVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31723) coarse/fine + T-CoT, [ME-Dex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21449) AE force/contact/change latent) but almost no **scaling curves**: what is the shape of downstream success vs auxiliary-loss weight, and does it peak and then collapse the way [HapTile](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04825) suggests (80% → 30% from a richer tactile representation)? A dose-response study with a modality-dropout schedule is currently unclaimed and is publishable as mechanism.

**O6. Modality dropout / tactile-ablation invariance as a designed property.**
[UniTacVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31723) explicitly asks Q5 "can we deploy on robots *without* tactile sensors at inference", and [VTAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23481) shows modality collapse. But no work optimizes a *tactile-dropout curriculum* jointly with a task-level target and reports the invariance/performance trade-off. Mechanism-level and cheap.

## 4. Best unoccupied opportunity + biggest prior-art risk

**Best opportunity — "Outcome-margin tactile world models" (O1 + O4, with O3 as the distinctive head).**
Build a tactile WAM whose heads predict, per future step and per candidate chunk: P(success), remaining irreversible-damage margin, and recoverability horizon. Use current/predicted touch only as evidence. Deploy as (i) a candidate ranker that replaces the hand-tuned force guard and (ii) a training-time auxiliary that shapes the shared latent — mirroring the [TacSushi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19613) train/deploy asymmetry (which is the single most effective auxiliary-loss result in this survey: 36.7% → 68.3% ID) but with a **task-level output consumed online**, which TacSushi explicitly does not do.
Why it wins: it is mechanism-level; it is not another routing mask; its target is provably not the tactile signal; and it directly attacks the three negative results ([TacPAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05266) one-third, [HiTac-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19574) 35.6% progress-only, [Compact VT-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09597) inconclusive pooled). The decisive experiment is a head-to-head at matched backbone/compute: future-tactile-latent target vs outcome-margin target, both with the same routing.

**Biggest prior-art risk — HiTac-WAM ([2608.19574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19574)).**
It already (a) forecasts a temporally resolved hierarchical tactile world state per candidate action chunk, (b) ranks candidates with a score that **includes a task-progress term**, (c) keeps the selected forecast as an online execution reference, and (d) triggers replanning on persistent forecast–observation deviation. A naive "we predict task progress from touch and use it to select actions" claim is therefore anticipated. Secondary risks: [TacSushi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19613) owns "progress + contact risk supervised from a tactile-fused latent (training-only)"; [Compact VT-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09597) owns "predict reward/force-budget from a visuotactile latent model and check whether it helps control"; [SCOTTI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25906) owns "progress prediction from tactile" (human domain). Novelty must be sharpened to: **calibrated, counterfactual, per-timestep outcome-margin/recoverability as a first-class online world-model output in robot manipulation, demonstrated to beat sensory-target prediction at matched compute.**

**Second-order risk:** the environment is moving fast (≈1–2 relevant arXiv preprints per month through 2026), and several groups (TARS/NUS, CASIA+ImprintX+BAAI, Fudan+NeoteAI, Li Auto, Ant Group) have obvious follow-up capacity. A 3–6 month window is realistic.
