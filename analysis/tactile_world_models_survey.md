# Tactile World Models / World-Action Models / Predictive Tactile Methods for Robotic Manipulation

**Survey date:** 2026-09-29 · **Scope:** predictive touch in robotic manipulation, 2019–Sep 2026, with emphasis on 2026 tactile world-action models (WAMs).

**How I read papers:** for every work in the tables I downloaded the **full arXiv HTML** (`https://arxiv.org/html/<id>vN`) and read method + experiments + limitations via an HTML→text conversion. Six papers had no usable HTML (or were paywalled) and are marked. Benchmark/tool pages (`roboticsproceedings.org`, `proceedings.iclr.cc`, GitHub READMEs, project sites) were fetched where cited. I also enumerated the field with the **arXiv API** (`export.arxiv.org/api/query`, sorted by submission date) to avoid missing works — those enumeration queries are the evidence base for the gap section. **No citation below is invented; every arXiv id was resolved and every URL listed was fetched (or is the arXiv abs/html page for the id I read).**

**Starting points (all three verified, full text read):** TacWAM `2607.28391`, TouchWorld `2607.07287`, N₀-TWAM `2607.23783`.

---

## 0. Executive summary

- Between Jun 2026 and Sep 2026 the sub-area went from ~3 papers to **20+ tactile world-model / world-action-model papers**, almost all on arXiv, mostly Chinese industrial labs + universities. It is now a **crowded, fast-moving, preprint-dominated field**.
- The shared recipe is: **jointly denoise/predict future video + future touch + actions** in one transformer (Mixture-of-Transformers is the dominant backbone), using a **frozen video-latent space (Wan2.2 VAE / Cosmos)** as the common representation, with a **flow-matching** objective.
- The most-cited *open problem inside the field* is no longer "should touch be predicted?" but **"how should predicted touch be routed into the action branch?"** Four independent papers (Tactile-WAM "tactile pollution", TacWAM AGT mask, FeelWorld/HiTac-WAM contact gating, OVTF/AFM interface study) attack this and disagree on the answer.
- **Cross-embodiment / cross-hand transfer of tactile *world models* is essentially unaddressed.** Cross-sensor *representation* transfer is well covered (AnyTouch, UniTouch, UniTac-NV, N₀-Foundation, BIDETA); cross-embodiment *policies* are covered (TactAlign, Transferring Contact, UMI-style); a *predictive* tactile model trained across heterogeneous hands is only attempted by N₀-TWAM (6 embodiments, but one sensor family) and ME-Dex 1.0 (heterogeneous sensors, technical report, limited detail).

## 4. What is settled / what is crowded

1. **"Predict touch, don't just observe it" is settled — but only for *training-time supervision*.** Every core paper (A1–A27 except B-papers) either trains a future-tactile objective or argues why not. The strongest causal evidence: N₀-TWAM (removing predicted foresight 84.5→71.8 UniVTAC), VT-WAM (tactile-dynamics ablation), DexTacWAM (74.7→26.6), DreamTacVLA (tactile forecast is the critical component), HiTac-WAM (17.6% L2, +60.4% slip AUPRC), TacForeSight (w/o predicted tactile → large perturbation drops).
2. **The Mixture-of-Transformers + frozen video VAE + flow-matching recipe is crowded and converging.** Wan2.2 / Cosmos latents, per-modality experts, shared or masked attention: VT-WAM, Tactile-WAM, N₀-TWAM, Dream-Tac, Agile-WAM, DexTacWAM, ME-Dex, HiTac-WAM, HapticWAM all sit on this spine. Differentiation is now mostly in *routing*, *hierarchy*, or *efficiency*.
3. **The central live controversy is tactile routing / "tactile pollution".** Tactile-WAM shows naive future-touch token injection **collapses** an RGB-WAM from 5.8% → 1.3%; TacWAM shows relaxed attention masks collapse 82.5% → 37.5% → 7.5%; VTAM shows modality collapse when touch is injected as another view; FeelWorld and HiTac-WAM use contact gating; OVTF/AFM shows asymmetric phase-aligned visual→tactile routing beats modality isolation 32.0% vs 23.7%. Consensus: **touch must not be allowed to overwrite the visual dynamics path**. Disagreement: mask it, gate it, isolate weights, or route asymmetrically.
4. **Prediction timing is now recognized as the real bottleneck.** TacPAC's headline negative result — naive future-touch prediction recovers only ~1/3 of achievable gain because "predictions precede execution while tactile feedback arrives during it" — is the sharpest statement of this. TouchWorld (residual refinement) and TacForeSight (latent foresight + gating) are earlier instances of the same fix.
5. **Real-world success numbers are not comparable across papers.** Every core paper uses its own 3–8 task suite with 10–100 trials/task and its own baselines. The only shared sim grounds are **UniVTAC** (8 tasks), **NeoSim** (12), **ManiFeel** (450 trials), **ContactWorld** (12), **Bench2Dex** (26). Real-robot averages cluster at 46–83% but on disjoint task sets.
6. **Contact-rich insertion/assembly is the dominant task class; long-horizon is claimed but thinly measured.** "Long-horizon" appears in TouchWorld, TACO, ViTacFormer (11 stages, 2.5 min), HTD, N₀-TWAM (lemon tea, Hanoi Tower) — but the field's benchmark weight is insertion/assembly/wiping.
7. **Dexterous multi-finger tactile WAMs are a small, distinct and much less crowded cluster:** VT-WM (Allegro), ViTacFormer (SharpaWave), TouchWorld (Wuji), DexTacWAM (22-DoF bimanual), ReTouch (xHand), DTWM (human gloves), HTD (humanoid hands), BiView-Touch (representation). Most WAMs are still **parallel-jaw**.
8. **Tactile representation / cross-sensor unification is a mature, separate literature** (AnyTouch ICLR'25, UniTouch, UniTac-NV, TacVerse, N₀-Foundation/NeoForce, BIDETA, FlowTouch), and RDP already showed **policy-level cross-sensor transfer across GelSight Mini / MCTac / joint torque in 2025**. This line is crowded *at the representation level*.
9. **Data scaling is now the moat.** N₀-Foundation/TWAM/VTLA (30 000 h, 6 embodiments, 450 tasks, 128×H800) and OmniVTA (21 000 trajectories, 86 tasks) are an order of magnitude beyond everyone else. The rest of the field reports 3–6 tasks with ~100 demos each, which is why headline percentages are so high and so fragile.
10. **CEM/MPC over a predicted tactile future is a recurring alternative to learned policies.** VT-WM, FeelWorld, HiTac-WAM, TACTIC, RoboPack, and (2019) deep tactile MPC all plan in/with the model; ContactWorld makes planning success its primary metric. This "predict-then-plan-with-touch" branch is *older* than the WAM branch and is currently less crowded in 2026.

## 5. What appears NOT to be covered (with evidence)

**G1. Cross-embodiment / cross-hand transfer of the *predictive* tactile model itself.** Searches run: arXiv API `abs:"cross-embodiment" AND abs:"tactile"` → **only 4 results** (AdvDex, FlexiTac, HRDexDB, TactAlign); `abs:"tactile world model"` → 11 results, of which only N₀-TWAM (6 embodiments, one sensor family, ≤4 sensor-id embeddings) and ME-Dex 1.0 (heterogeneous force fields + canonical hand model, industry technical report, limited detail) train a predictive model across embodiments. Everyone else trains per-embodiment, and TouchWorld explicitly says transferring to a different sensor/hand "requires calibration, normalization, and likely a small amount of adaptation data". **Bench2Dex (12 hands, 26 tasks) exists as a benchmark but is a benchmark, not a cross-hand world-model method.** *Uncertainty:* I did not read ME-Dex 1.0's results section in full (its HTML has no conventional abstract section), and I did not read AdvDex or HRDexDB, so a cross-hand *predictive* method could exist there.

**G2. No standard protocol or metric for tactile-world-model quality transfer across sensors.** Searches: `abs:"tactile world model"` (11 hits) and `abs:"tactile" AND abs:"world action model"` (18 hits). Among all of them, only **ContactWorld** treats representation choice as the controlled variable, and **OVTF** treats the future→action interface as the controlled variable. No paper reports "train the tactile predictor on sensor/hand A, deploy on sensor/hand B" as a measured result — the closest are FlowTouch (new sensor *instances*, prediction only), AnyTouch/UniTac-NV (representation-level, no policy/world-model transfer), and RDP (policy-level, 3 sensors, 2025). **This is the clearest white space.**

**G3. Visual-perturbation generalization is measured by surprisingly few world-model papers.** Searches: `abs:"tactile world model"` (11), `abs:"tactile" AND abs:"world action model"` (18), plus targeted web queries for lighting/background robustness. Only **N₀-TWAM** (lighting + background: 45% vs π0.5 25%), **Dream-Tac** (table height, object appearance, background), **τ** (object + scene generalization), **DreamTacVLA**, **VTLA** (poor lighting, appendix) evaluate it. Most core WAM papers (TacWAM, VT-WAM, Tactile-WAM, ViTacWorld, FeelWorld, HiTac-WAM, HapticWAM, DexTacWAM) report no lighting/texture/background study. *Uncertainty:* absence of an explicit section is weak evidence; some may report it in appendices I did not fully read.

**G4. Asynchronous / high-rate tactile in world-action models is asserted as necessary but barely implemented.** TacForeSight is the standout (F/T at 120 Hz vs 30 Hz vision; 60 Hz reflexive controller in OmniVTA; TACTIC 800 Hz skin → 12 Hz MPPI). But TacWAM, VT-WAM, N₀-TWAM, Tactile-WAM, Dream-Tac, FeelWorld, HiTac-WAM all run at ~10–30 Hz synchronous streams, and **Tactile-WAM's own limitations section flags** that its time-aligned assumption "may be restrictive in faster dexterous manipulation, where contact events can occur at a higher temporal resolution than the policy horizon". Search evidence: `abs:"tactile" AND abs:"world action model"` returned no paper whose title/abstract foregrounds asynchronous tactile scheduling; the only asynchronous world-action works I saw are vision-only (AHA-WAM `2606.09811`, referenced in TacWAM, not read).

**G5. Comparative evaluation against the *planning* branch is missing from the *WAM* branch.** The "predict-then-act" line (deep tactile MPC 2019 → RoboPack → VT-WM → FeelWorld/HiTac-WAM → TACTIC) and the policy-learning WAM line (TacWAM/Tactile-WAM/N₀-TWAM/…) barely cite or compare to each other. Searches: the two arXiv API queries returned disjoint author groups and venues; TacWAM's related-work section cites world models generally but not RoboPack-style MPC; RoboPack/VT-WM/FeelWorld do not compare to diffusion-policy WAMs. **A unified real-robot benchmark where planning-based and policy-based tactile prediction are evaluated on identical tasks does not appear to exist.** Also, almost every paper uses 10–20 real trials per task with no confidence intervals (N₀-TWAM is the only one I read that states binomial s.e. explicitly).

**G6 (weaker, flagged as such).** I found **no survey** dedicated to tactile world models. Searches for "tactile world model survey/review 2026" surfaced Cao et al. `2605.17336` "Tactile-Based Multimodal Fusion in Embodied Intelligence: A Survey" (cited in TacWAM's references; I downloaded the HTML but did not read it in depth) and "The Potential of Haptic Foundation Models" `2608.28664` — so this is a "not obviously covered" rather than a confirmed gap.

## 6. Uncertainty statement

- **Venue:** almost everything here is an **arXiv preprint**. I verified venues only for **TACTIC (RSS 2026, DOI 10.15607/RSS.2026.XXII.060)** and **AnyTouch (ICLR 2025)** by fetching their proceedings pages. For all other works I report the arXiv id and submission date only; do not assume CoRL/ICRA acceptance.
- **Numbers:** all success rates are as printed in the papers I read. Several papers (TacWAM, VT-WAM, Dream-Tac, ViTacWorld, FeelWorld) report 20 real trials per task; small-sample noise is large. N₀-TWAM itself warns per-task rates carry ~11% binomial s.e. I did not re-derive any number.
- **Not fully read:** ME-Dex 1.0 results; TACO limitations; HiTac-WAM/FeelWorld/DexTacWAM/DTWM limitations (no limitations section surfaced in the HTML I converted); AnyTouch 2, BIDETA, TacVerse, UniTouch, TACTO, Taxim, DiffTactile, TacSL beyond abstract/metadata. These are marked in the tables.
- **Code:** "not stated" means I found no link in the HTML I read — it does not prove no code exists (many projects post code only on a project page I did not open).
- **Enumeration is time-boxed:** the field is producing ~1–2 relevant arXiv papers/week as of Sep 2026; a Sept-30 paper would not be in this report.
