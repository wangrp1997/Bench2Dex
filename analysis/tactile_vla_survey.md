**Compiled:** 2026-09-29 · **Method:** web_search + web_fetch of full HTML papers (arxiv.org/html, ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org) plus project pages and one OpenReview/ECCV PDF landing page. Every URL below was actually fetched by me unless explicitly marked *[index-only — not fetched]*.

**Verification status legend**
- ✅ = full paper text fetched and read (numbers taken from the paper)
- 🟡 = landing page / project page / PDF index fetched; paper body not fully read

## 0. Headline answers to the four questions

| Question | Answer | Strongest evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Adds tactile to GR00T N1.5?** | **YES — exactly one paper.** | MoSS / "Modular Sensory Stream", [arXiv 2604.23272](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23272v2). Fine-tunes **GR00T N1.5** with tactile *and* joint-torque streams; GR00T N1.5 baseline 20.8% → 42.7% (tactile) → 49.0% (tactile+torque) avg over 4 real tasks. Also applies Tactile-VLA and ForceVLA onto GR00T N1.5 as baselines (30.2%, 34.4%). |
| **Adds tactile to π0.5?** | **YES, several.** | TacVLA, [arXiv 2603.12665](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12665v1) — LoRA-finetunes OpenPI `pi05_base` + tactile array tokens + contact gating. STAR, [arXiv 2609.12549](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.12549v1) — initializes from **π0.5** weights. T-Rex, [arXiv 2606.17055](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17055v1) — uses **π0.5 and π0.5+tactile** as baselines. HapTile, [arXiv 2606.04825](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04825v1) — fine-tunes π0 with tactile. |
| **Bimanual dexterous hands (not grippers)?** | **YES, a small 2026 cluster.** | T-Rex (bimanual, 2× 22-DoF Sharpa Wave hands, 100 h data, 12 tasks); STAR (bimanual 10-DoF hands, 268 taxels/hand, 200 h, 65 tasks); ME-Dex 1.0 (Xynova Flex2 dexterous hands **and** grippers); HATO/Lin et al. 2024 (2× Psyonic Ability Hands, 60 touch sensors, but no language conditioning). |
| **Tactile for invariance to visual nuisance?** | **PARTIAL — robustness is studied, formal invariance is not.** | ManiFeel ([2505.18472](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18472v2)) is the only *systematic* study (dim-light sorting, closed-box search, viewpoint ablation). TacVLA block-camera occlusion ≈30%→>60%; ForceVLA occlusion 90%; 3D-ViTac occlusion-vs-#cameras ablation. **No paper found that frames touch as giving invariance to photometric nuisance (lighting/texture/background) while preserving task-geometry equivariance.** |
| **Cross-embodiment tactile fusion?** | **YES, exactly one clear paper, brand new.** | **ME-Dex 1.0**, [arXiv 2609.21449](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21449v1) — *Canonical Hand Model* maps grippers **and** dexterous hands of different finger structures onto a shared finger/palm region template, plus a *Unified Tactile Autoencoder* into a shared latent. TaF-VLA is cross-**sensor** (6 different tactile sensors), not cross-morphology. |

## (b) Crowded vs. open

**Crowded (saturated — do not build a novelty claim here):**
- **"Bolt a tactile encoder onto π0 / OpenVLA and concatenate tactile tokens into the VLM prefix."** This is now the default recipe and has been done many times: Tactile-VLA (single fused MLP token into π0), OmniVTLA (256 tactile tokens into π0), ForceVLA (force token after the VLM + MoE), TacVLA (36 tactile tokens into π0.5 + gating), HapTile (V+T for π0 and DP). *Novelty must come from the fusion mechanism, the inductive bias, or the data — not from "we added touch to a VLA."*
- **Single-arm parallel gripper, peg/USB/plug insertion, 1–2 sensors, successful-rate table vs a vision-only baseline.** TacFiLM, ForceVLA, VLA-Touch, TacVLA, Tactile-VLA, TaF-VLA, HapTile, OmniVTLA all occupy this space. Reported gains land in the +15 to +45 point range over a vision-only or naive-concat baseline.
- **"Naive concatenation wastes tokens / hurts under distribution shift."** Now empirically demonstrated at least twice: ForceVLA (MoE-before-VLM = 0%, concat-after-VLM 60% < MoE 80%) and TacFiLM (Cross-Attn 48% < Concat 71% < FiLM 87%). Late/post-VLM fusion is established as safer than early fusion.
- **"Tactile is sparse, so gate it."** TacVLA (threshold-based contact gating + attention masking), STAR (contact-gated sparse token selection), T-Rex (variable-rate high-frequency expert), MoSS (two-stage + future prediction), HapTile (marker tracking vs raw images). Several independent groups converged on gating in 2026.
- **Predicting *future* tactile/force as an auxiliary loss.** MoSS, STAR, T-Rex, ME-Dex (tactile expert as a future-prediction expert), VT-WAM, Motus2. This is now standard.
- **Tactile-language models for planning (Octopi-style linguistic tactile feedback).** VLA-Touch is the reference; the space is thin but the idea is claimed.
- **Peg-in-hole with a GelSight/DIGIT + Franka.** Extremely crowded.

**Open / thin (defensible gaps):**
- **Tactile for photometric invariance as a *stated, measured* objective.** Widespread motivation ("touch is invariant to lighting and viewpoint" is repeated in HapTile's intro and others), but I found **no paper that measures policy performance under lighting/texture/background/camera-pose shifts and attributes the gain to touch**, nor one that separates *invariance to nuisance* from *equivariance to task geometry*. ManiFeel is the closest infrastructure but frames it as "visually challenging tasks", not invariance.
- **Cross-morphology tactile fusion (one model, many hands with different finger structures and sensor layouts).** Exactly one paper: ME-Dex 1.0 (Canonical Hand Model + Unified Tactile Autoencoder, Sept 2026). TaF-VLA does cross-*sensor* transfer; OmniVTLA handles force-vs-vision sensor heterogeneity; neither does cross-*hand*. STAR shares one tactile encoder between left and right hands but does not vary morphology. **This looks like the most open high-value direction.**
- **Tactile on *anthropomorphic bimanual dexterous hands* with full taxel coverage.** Only T-Rex, STAR, ME-Dex (2026), plus HATO (2024, no language). Notably absent: anything on GR00T N1.5 / π0.5 / RDT with a *dexterous* hand and tactile — MoSS covers GR00T N1.5 but its dexterous-hand result is a separate "applicability" section, and TacVLA/ForceVLA/VLA-Touch/OmniVTLA are gripper-first.
- **Tactile transfer to RDT / RT-2 / Groot-family backbones beyond GR00T N1.5.** RDT appears only as the frozen base in VLA-Touch (action-chunk refinement, no tactile in the backbone). **No work found that puts touch inside RDT-1B/RDT-2.**
- **Tactile + 3D/geometry-preserving fusion.** 3D-ViTac (2024) is still essentially alone in fusing touch into a *shared 3D space*; ME-Dex uses a canonical hand template but works in force-field space. Combining explicit 3D geometry with a modern VLA is open.
- **Systematic negative results / when touch hurts.** HapTile is the only paper I read that reports a case where adding touch *degraded* performance (DP + marker tracking: wipe 80%→30%, pour 50%→20%). This is under-reported and is a real contribution opportunity.
- **Benchmarks with controllable visual nuisance.** ManiFeel (dim light, occlusion, viewpoint) is the only one; it is 2025/2026 and explicit "invariance" evaluation is not its framing.

## (c) Explicit gap evidence — what I searched, what came back

### Gap 1 — "Adds tactile to GR00T N1.5 specifically"
- **Queries:** `GR00T N1.5 tactile sensor fine-tuning touch` · `"GR00T" tactile force touch sensor fine-tune N1.5 manipulation` · `pi0.5 tactile touch VLA physical intelligence` · `RDT-2 tactile force robot diffusion transformer`.
- **What came back:** a hit on `arxiv.org/pdf/2604.23272` containing the table fragment `GR00T N1.5 + MoSS (Ours) | Unstack Cup 54.2 | PnP Egg 66.7`. I then fetched the full paper: **MoSS = "Modular Sensory Stream for Integrating Physical Feedback in Vision-Language-Action Models"**, KAIST+RLWRLD, [arXiv 2604.23272v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23272v2). Confirmed: it augments **GR00T N1.5** (and π0) with tactile + torque streams on the action expert, with a full baseline table.
- **Also:** no other paper surfaced for GR00T + tactile. Note OmniVTLA's own Table 1 classifies VTLA/VTLA-Tactile methods but does **not** mention GR00T. STAR uses GR00T **N1.7** as a *vision-only/dexterous baseline* (0% and 8%), not tactile-augmented.
- **Conclusion:** exactly one paper (MoSS), published ~Sept 2026 — i.e., this is **very recently opened, not crowded**. It is a gripper+dexterous "applicability" claim, not a dexterous-hand-first paper.

### Gap 2 — "Adds tactile to π0.5 specifically"
- **Queries:** `pi0.5 tactile touch VLA physical intelligence` · `pi0.5 tactile touch fine-tuning contact-rich`.
- **What came back:** TacVLA explicitly ("LoRA ... on top of an OpenPI backbone checkpoint (`pi05_base`)", "+ Tactile ... following the Pi0.5 design"). STAR initializes "with the π0.5 weights". T-Rex lists `π0.5` and `π0.5 + tactile` as baselines. HapTile fine-tunes π0 (not π0.5).
- **Conclusion:** **crowded** for π0-family + touch on grippers; **open** for π0.5 + dexterous hands (only STAR/T-Rex touch this, and both are Sept/Jun 2026).

### Gap 3 — "Bimanual dexterous hands"
- **Queries:** `bimanual dexterous hand tactile VLA 2026` · `dexterous hand tactile vision language action` · `Learning visuotactile skills with two multifingered hands Lin arXiv 2404.16823` · `bimanual tactile manipulation policy learning` · `multi-finger tactile transformer policy` · `in-hand manipulation tactile VLA`.
- **What came back:** T-Rex (2606.17055) — bimanual Dexmate Vega-1, 2× 22-DoF Sharpa Wave, 12 tasks; STAR (2609.12549) — bimanual 10-DoF hands, 268 taxels/hand, 65 tasks; ME-Dex (2609.21449) — Xynova Flex2 dexterous hands; HATO (2404.16823) — 2× Psyonic Ability Hands with 60 touch sensors (no language).
- **Explicit quotation from T-Rex §2:** *"existing work largely focuses on single-arm or parallel-gripper embodiments."* From STAR §1: *"Current real-world dexterous hand datasets mainly focus on simple skills such as pick-and-place."*
- **Conclusion:** **this gap closed in mid-2026** but is only ~3 papers deep, all with very different architectures. Very live and fast-moving — a strong place to work, but check for weekly new arXiv postings.

### Gap 4 — "Tactile for invariance to visual nuisance while keeping geometry equivariance"
- **Queries:** `tactile sensing invariance to lighting background visual nuisance manipulation policy` · `tactile invariance camera pose viewpoint manipulation policy learning` · `tactile sensing helps robustness to lighting background appearance changes robot policy` · `touch invariant representation viewpoint robot manipulation 2026` · `visuotactile policy domain randomization lighting` · `tactile complement vision occlusion`.
- **What came back (robustness, not invariance):**
  - ManiFeel [2505.18472](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18472v2) — dim-lighting ball sorting, closed-box object search, occluded peg reorientation, and a **camera-viewpoint ablation** in Appendix D-D. This is the strongest existing infrastructure for the question.
  - TacVLA [2603.12665](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12665v1) — physically blocking the front camera: avg ~30% → >60%. In-box picking 10% → 70%.
  - ForceVLA [2505.22159](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2505.22159) — "Visual Occlusion" 90% (vs 30–60% for baselines).
  - 3D-ViTac [2410.24091](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24091v2) — occlusion ablation by varying the number of cameras; tactile points preserve whole-task performance.
  - HapTile [2606.04825](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04825v1) — cites "invariant to lighting and viewpoint" as motivation, but its own Limitations say the dataset **lacks lighting/background variation**.
  - HATO [2404.16823](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16823v1) — rare-initialization robustness (touch 10/10 vs 4/10 without). But the authors state policies learned from scratch are "susceptible to appearance changes in the scene" — i.e., **touch did not solve appearance robustness**.
  - 2404.16823 additionally faces an explicit negative: with touch, the "3rd-view-only" camera policy scored 0/10 — so **vision is not replaceable** here.
- **What did NOT come back:** any paper with a *controlled photometric-nuisance* protocol (vary lighting color/intensity, texture, background clutter, camera extrinsics) and a touch-vs-no-touch comparison. No paper uses the words invariance/equivariance in this decomposition.
- **Conclusion:** **genuinely open.** The motivation is ubiquitous in intros; the measurement is missing. Note the honest counter-evidence (HATO) that touch alone does not fix appearance shift.

### Gap 5 — "Cross-embodiment tactile fusion"
- **Queries:** `cross-embodiment tactile fusion different hands sensor layouts one model` · `tactile sensing cross-embodiment generalization robot hand morphology` · `cross-embodiment tactile` · `one policy many robot hands tactile` · `heterogeneous tactile sensors foundation model` · `unified tactile representation across sensors`.
- **What came back:**
  - **ME-Dex 1.0** [2609.21449](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21449v1) — the only paper with an explicit **Canonical Hand Model** mapping grippers and dexterous hands of different structures to a shared finger/palm region template, + a **Unified Tactile Autoencoder** with shared parameters and region/pooling-type embeddings. Trained jointly on RoboTwin (grippers), DexJoCo (dexterous hands), ManiFeel (grippers), Xynova Flex2 (dexterous), SO-101 (gripper).
  - **TaF-VLA** [2601.20321](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20321v1) — cross-**sensor** (4 custom VBTS + 2 GelSight Mini) with a "Cross-Sensor Generalization (Unseen Sensors)" experiment; explicitly argues latent alignment beats explicit force regression for new sensors. **Morphology is fixed.**
  - **OmniVTLA** [2508.08706](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2508.08706) — dual tactile encoder to absorb *force-based vs vision-based* sensor heterogeneity; but the gripper and the 4-finger hand experiments are separate pipelines, not one cross-embodiment model.
  - **MoSS** [2604.23272](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23272v2) — has §4.4 "Applicability to Different Embodiment" with gripper + hand and distinct sensor configs; the design (mirror the action expert, swap streams) is *architecturally* embodiment-agnostic, but full numbers were truncated in the HTML I fetched.
  - Older tactile rep-learning lines (Sparsh, T3 [2406.13640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13640), AnyTouch, UniTouch, Sparsh-X) unify across **sensor types and tasks**, not across **hand morphology**.
- **Conclusion:** **essentially one paper** (ME-Dex, Sept 2026). Highest-value open gap.

### Searched and found nothing (honest negatives)

- **RDT + tactile inside the backbone** — the only RDT×touch work found is VLA-Touch, which leaves RDT **frozen** and refines its action chunks with an external interpolant controller. No paper integrates touch into RDT-1B/2 itself.

## (d) Priority reading list before writing a related-work section

**Tier 1 — must read in full (they define the current state of the art and the specific gaps):**
1. **ME-Dex 1.0** — [arXiv 2609.21449](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21449v1). The cross-embodiment tactile paper. Read §3.3 (Canonical Hand Model, Unified Tactile AE) and §4.4 carefully; get the numbers I could not extract.

3. **TacFiLM** — [arXiv 2603.14604](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14604v1) + [ECCV page](https://eccv.eventhosts.cc/virtual/2026/poster/5973) + [project](https://charliem7.github.io/projects/TacFilm/). 8. **Tactile-VLA** — [arXiv 2507.09160](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2507.09160). Token-level fusion + hybrid position-force control + CoT reasoning; the most-cited "physical knowledge in the VLM" framing.

10. **TaF-VLA** — [arXiv 2601.20321](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20321v1). Tactile→**force** alignment (rather than tactile→vision), VQ codebooks, and cross-*sensor* generalization.
11. **3D-ViTac** — [arXiv 2410.24091](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24091v2). The geometry-preserving 3D fusion baseline and the occlusion/resolution ablations.
12. **VLA-Touch** — [arXiv 2507.17294](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.17294v1). The "don't touch the base VLA" modular alternative; linguistic tactile feedback for planning.
13. **OmniVTLA** — [arXiv 2508.08706](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2508.08706). Semantic-aligned tactile encoder + ObjTac dataset + a gripper/dexterous comparison inside one paper.
14. **HATO / two multifingered hands** — [arXiv 2404.16823](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16823v1). Pre-VLA bimanual dexterous visuotactile; important as the "before" state and for the honest appearance-robustness caveat.
